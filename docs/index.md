<div class="section-block" id="about-waliin">
  <span class="section-label">ABOUT THE PRODUCT</span>
  <h2>Introducing Waliin</h2>
  <p>
    Developed by Solvya, Waliin supports the management of medical
    equipment throughout its lifecycle in healthcare facilities.
    It connects clinical staff, biomedical engineers and health
    managers through a coordinated equipment management process.
  </p>
  <p>
    The platform supports equipment registration, fault reporting,
    assessment, repair coordination, verification and maintenance
    reporting.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="section-block" id="workflow">
  <span class="section-label">HOW IT WORKS</span>
  <h2>One platform. Four key stages.</h2>
  <p>
    Waliin brings the main activities involved in healthcare equipment
    management into one connected workflow.
  </p>

  <div class="workflow-grid">
    <div class="workflow-card">
      <span class="step-number">01</span>
      <h3>Equipment Registration</h3>
      <p>
        Biomedical engineers register medical equipment, record
        equipment details, assign departments and maintain
        equipment records.
      </p>
      <img
        src="assets/images/biomedical_engineer.png"
        alt="Biomedical engineer registering medical equipment"
        loading="lazy"
        style="width:100%;height:360px;object-fit:contain;border-radius:8px;"
      >
    </div>

    <div class="workflow-card">
      <span class="step-number">02</span>
      <h3>Fault Reporting</h3>
      <p>
        Clinical staff report equipment faults and follow their
        progress through the equipment management process.
      </p>
      <img
        src="assets/images/doctor.png"
        alt="Clinical staff reporting medical equipment faults"
        loading="lazy"
        style="width:100%;height:360px;object-fit:contain;border-radius:8px;"
      >
    </div>

    <div class="workflow-card">
      <span class="step-number">03</span>
      <h3>Assessment and Repair</h3>
      <p>
        Biomedical engineers assess reported faults, document
        repair activities and record the work performed on
        medical equipment.
      </p>
      <img
        src="assets/images/doctor_and_biomedical.png"
        alt="Clinical staff and biomedical engineer discussing equipment"
        loading="lazy"
        style="width:100%;height:360px;object-fit:contain;border-radius:8px;"
      >
    </div>

    <div class="workflow-card">
      <span class="step-number">04</span>
      <h3>Verification and Reporting</h3>
      <p>
        Equipment repair activities can be verified, while health
        managers oversee equipment information, review maintenance
        activity and access reports.
      </p>
      <img
        src="assets/images/health_manager.png"
